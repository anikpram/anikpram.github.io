About Me: Hi! I'm Aniket Pramanik, a Research Fellow at the Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Research Center focusing on MRI‑guided radiotherapy, computational MRI and AI/ML. I build fast, robust reconstruction methods and learning‑based systems for treatment guidance. Prior to that, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Oak Ridge National Laboratory where I worked on reconstruction methods for high-resolution CT imaging of additively manufactured parts. I obtained my PhD from the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Iowa working under the supervision of Prof. Mathews Jacob.
